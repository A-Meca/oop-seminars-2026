## How the seminars work

- Each week's exercises are seminar workflow are published before the seminar. Try them before you come: in the seminar we work through them together and discuss your solutions.
- Sample Solutions are published after all the groups have finished the seminar.
- Your own work goes in your own repository, committed and pushed at the end of every seminar.
